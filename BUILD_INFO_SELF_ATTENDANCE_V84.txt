Security guarantees shipped with this build:
- Identity-bound: every row in the section is filtered by the signed-in account's
  linked member id (account.memberId). No URL, query string, or control can widen
  the scope.
- Read-only by construction: the section renders no editable field, and the
  module calls no write API. The permission is a module (view) grant with no
  matching action, so a role holding it receives zero shared-database write
  columns.
- Role-locked: only Trainee/Probationary may hold the module. Enforced in the
  Supabase read/save functions, in role-access-v6.js, and in the Role &
  Permission Center (the toggle is locked for every other role).
- Fail-closed: without the grant the navigation item disappears, the shell
  refuses to open the section, and the section stays hidden in the DOM.
